**How the system works**
- **ESMA's database:** ESMA maintains a public online database listing EuVECA managers, the funds they label that way, and the countries where those funds are marketed.
- **Registration is national:** Registration is handled by national authorities. The rules define a competent authority as the body each home country designates to register managers. (Article 3(m) defines it as the national authority the home Member State designates, by law or regulation, to undertake the registration of managers.)
- **Applications go to the fund's regulator:** Managers apply to register each fund they want to label EuVECA, and the application is made to the competent authority of the qualifying venture capital fund.
- **Information reaches ESMA:** Information gathered under the process is made available to the fund's competent authority, to host Member States' authorities, and to ESMA.
- **Notices:** The 2014 implementing rules chose email for notifications about passport events and removals, since no purpose-built ESMA system existed at the time. (ESMA had not yet developed a dedicated IT tool for that notification, so e-mail was judged the most appropriate format.)
